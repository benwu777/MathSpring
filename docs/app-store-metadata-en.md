## Description

MathSpring is an offline learning app built around mathematical thinking. Choose question packs by grade range, topic, and learning goal, then work through arithmetic, geometry, algebra, number theory, combinatorics, and logic problems with explanations.

Each learner keeps their own practice and quiz history, so you can revisit questions, answers, and solutions. Question packs and learning records stay on the device and work offline, so focused practice can continue anywhere.

Highlights:

- Grade-range and topic-based question packs
- Practice and quiz flows
- Figures, choices, and answer review
- On-device progress and history for each learner
- Traditional Chinese, English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese interfaces
- No account required to start offline practice

Free and full versions:

- Free: answer every question and see the answers, choose 3 questions a day to view full solutions, and create 1 learner.
- Full version (one-time purchase, no subscription): full solutions for every question, all paper-doll styles, and up to 5 learners.

## App Review Notes

No sign-in or account creation is required; all question packs and learning records are stored on the device and work offline. One optional non-consumable in-app purchase, "Full Unlock", is available; there is no subscription. The free version lets users answer every question and see the answers, view full solutions for 3 self-chosen questions per day, and create 1 learner. The purchase unlocks every solution, all paper-doll styles, and up to 5 learners. Purchase and Restore Purchases are in the Settings tab, as are the support and privacy links, which open the browser only when the user taps them.
