# Boy short hair using girl fit reference

## Goal

Create one accepted natural-short-hair family for the five boy face shapes by
using the approved girl avatar head proportions and hair-fit silhouette as a
reference, while retaining male faces and a cleaner male haircut language.

## Direction

- Retain boy face, eyes, nose, mouth, and skin artwork.
- Reference the approved girl system only for crown arc, forehead hairline,
  temple-to-ear connection, and canonical-canvas placement.
- Draw a cleaner natural boy short haircut with fewer, more deliberate hair
  strokes than the girl assets.  Reject cap-like rims, mushroom volume,
  disconnected side tufts, and eye-covering fringe.
- Make one fixed hair layer for every `boy_face_01` through `boy_face_05`.
  They share the same hairstyle family but differ in part direction, fringe,
  crown texture, and temple closure as needed to fit their actual head shape.

## Acceptance

Inspect all five at main-preview and tile sizes.  The crown must meet the head
continuously, the side hair must terminate naturally by the ears, the hairline
must clear eyebrows, and each face-specific template must be recognizably
different without becoming a different broad hairstyle.
