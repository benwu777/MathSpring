# Olympiad Content Policy

## Goal

Define how MathSpring should source, rewrite, classify, and ship olympiad-style question content without confusing internal references, public-domain material, and licensed competition problems.

## Source Tiers

### 1. Official Competition References

These are authoritative materials used to understand:

- grade grouping
- question mix
- scoring style
- answer format
- competition tone

Examples include official Math Kangaroo and AMC public reference materials, sample structures, answer-sheet rules, and published problem formats.

These references are for alignment and editorial benchmarking unless explicit reuse rights have been confirmed.

### 2. App-Writable Olympiad-Style Content

This is the default content tier for MathSpring.

Questions in this tier may be:

- internally authored
- AI-assisted and then human-reviewed
- rewritten from competition-style inspirations without copying protected wording

This tier should preserve olympiad characteristics:

- short, clean statements
- logical traps
- non-routine reasoning
- age-appropriate competition pacing
- concise but explicit solution steps

### 3. Licensed Or Explicitly Permitted Competition Content

This tier is reserved for future use if the project obtains clear permission to include official past problems or adapted official materials inside the app.

No content should be placed in this tier unless the usage terms have been reviewed and recorded.

## Elementary-First Strategy

For the current product stage:

- elementary learners should align first to Math Kangaroo-style grouping
- competition labels should be shown in learner-facing profile language such as `Kangaroo 1-2`, `Kangaroo 3-4`, and `Kangaroo 5-6`
- practice and quiz sessions should draw from olympiad-style question banks designed for these groupings

Later expansion may add older-learner pathways aligned to AMC-style competition structure.

## Rights Boundary

MathSpring must not assume that official olympiad or competition problems can be bundled into the app simply because they are visible online.

Before shipping official past-paper content, confirm:

- whether the original wording may be reproduced
- whether diagrams may be reproduced
- whether translations may be made
- whether commercial or app distribution is allowed
- whether attribution alone is sufficient

Until those rights are confirmed, MathSpring should ship rewritten olympiad-style content rather than direct copies of official competition problems.

## Editorial Rules For App Content

Every app-shippable question should include:

- prompt
- answer
- stepwise solution
- difficulty
- topic
- subtopic
- answer mode
- figure definition when needed
- source classification metadata in the editorial workflow

Recommended editorial source labels:

- `reference_official`
- `rewritten_internal`
- `licensed_official`

These labels do not need to be shown in the learner UI, but they should exist in the content workflow so the project can track provenance safely.
